# michi — career service LP concept

自主制作の架空キャリア支援サービスLP。実際の求人・予約・利用実績ではありません。

- Live preview: https://kazumateraokashoten-spec.github.io/career-lp/
- Design rationale: design-note.html
- Stack: static HTML/CSS/JS, no runtime dependencies or analytics.
- Consultation UI is browser-memory-only; no data transmission or persistence.
- Native dialog, keyboard-operable tabs and reduced-motion support.
- Image: assets/counseling.webp, AI-generated for this concept. Original retained outside repository. Not a real adviser/client endorsement.

## Image generation prompt

Create a premium editorial commercial photograph for a fictional Japanese career counseling landing page, landscape 3:2. In a light airy modern Tokyo office with pale blue wall and soft daylight, a friendly Japanese female career counselor around 32 in a light blue shirt listens warmly to a Japanese woman around 27 wearing an ivory blouse. Both naturally smiling lightly in candid conversation, not looking at camera, seated at pale oak round table, notebook and glass of water, subtle plant to right. Upper body portraits, candid authentic welcoming atmosphere, natural skin texture, professional advertising photography, balanced high-key natural lighting, blue/white/cream palette, sophisticated understated styling. Subjects on center and right, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no distorted hands. No exaggerated stock-photo thumbs-up or handshakes. High resolution.
